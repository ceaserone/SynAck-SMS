SynAck SMS is an Android SMS/MMS application maintained by SynAck Networks and based on the open-source QUIK/QKSMS project.

Features:
- SMS and carrier MMS messaging
- Scheduled and delayed messages
- Message backup and restore
- Local number blocking, filters, and archiving
- Speech-to-text and text-to-speech capabilities
- Attachments, notifications, and conversation management

Encrypted SynAck-to-SynAck messages are planned, not yet implemented.

Source code and issues: https://github.com/ceaserone/SynAck-SMS
